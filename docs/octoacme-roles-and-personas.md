# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA and Testing Leads own the quality strategy and ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release. They partner with developers and product teams to define testability requirements and validate solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Define and execute test plans (unit, integration, end-to-end, security)
- Validate features against acceptance criteria
- Report quality metrics and blockers
- Collaborate on testability and design reviews
- Manage QA timelines and capacity
- Escalate quality risks to PM and Product Lead

### Goals
- Deliver defect-free features to production
- Reduce rework and regression risk
- Provide early feedback on design and implementation
- Enable fast, confident releases

### Typical Communication
- Weekly QA status in delivery syncs
- Acceptance criteria discussion during planning
- Quality reports in weekly stakeholder updates
- Escalation of critical defects and release blockers

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on testability during design and code reviews; provide feedback on implementation quality
- **Product Managers**: Partner on acceptance criteria definition and success metrics
- **Project Managers**: Report quality status and escalate blockers in delivery syncs
- **Technical Leads**: Work together on test strategy and quality requirements for complex features

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide strategic technical guidance, evaluate design trade-offs, and ensure solutions align with long-term architecture and maintainability goals. They mentor developers and manage technical risk.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve technical designs
- Evaluate architectural trade-offs and technical debt
- Mentor developers and conduct code/design reviews
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Ensure consistency with coding standards and best practices
- Guide refactoring and infrastructure improvements

### Goals
- Maintain code quality and system resilience
- Reduce technical debt and long-term maintenance burden
- Enable faster feature delivery through good architecture
- Foster a learning culture through mentorship

### Typical Communication
- Design review sessions during planning and development
- Mentorship during code reviews
- Architecture discussions in technical design docs
- Risk identification during planning and standups

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Mentor on design decisions; guide on technical best practices and code quality
- **Project Managers**: Identify and escalate technical risks and dependencies
- **QA/Testing Leads**: Advise on testability and technical approach to quality validation
- **Product Managers**: Provide technical input on feasibility and architectural implications of feature requests

---

## Sponsor / Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors and key stakeholders provide business context, approve scope and investment, and serve as the escalation authority for decisions affecting project strategy or resource allocation.

### Responsibilities
- Define business requirements and success metrics
- Approve project scope, timeline, and resource allocation
- Serve as escalation point for business-impacting risks or blockers
- Provide strategic input on trade-offs
- Communicate project outcomes to leadership
- Align project with organizational strategy

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value
- Unblock cross-org dependencies
- Maintain alignment with company strategy
- Inform leadership on progress and risks

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and demos
- Kickoff and major milestone reviews
- Escalation for strategic risks or scope changes
- Release announcements and impact reporting

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Receive regular status updates and escalations; provide decisions on scope and priority trade-offs
- **Product Managers**: Align on business requirements and success metrics
- **Delivery Team**: Provide business context and approval for major decisions affecting project strategy

- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Pay attention to interaction patterns to understand how roles collaborate and depend on each other throughout the project lifecycle.
